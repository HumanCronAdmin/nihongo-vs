Ojou-sama Speech (お嬢様)

How They Talk in Anime

The Ojou-sama character type is a refined, wealthy young lady known for her elegant speech and polished demeanor, often using formal language to convey a sense of sophistication and poise. She typically comes from a high-society background and is well-versed in etiquette and manners. Her speech patterns reflect her upbringing, with a focus on propriety and courtesy.

Speech Patterns

First Person わたくし (watakushi)
Sentence Endings です (desu), であります (de arimasu), ございます (gozaimasu), ます (masu)
Signature Phrases よろしくお願いします (yoroshiku onegaishimasu) - Please treat me well, お世話になります (osewa ni narimasu) - I am in your care, ごめんなさい (gomen nasai) - Excuse me/Sorry
Characteristics
  • use of honorific language
  • polite vocabulary
  • formal sentence structure
  • avoidance of contractions and colloquialisms
Formality
very formal

Example Dialogues

Introducing herself to a new acquaintance

わたくしは鈴木のお嬢様です

Watakushi wa Suzuki no ojou-sama desu

I am Suzuki's young lady

Responding to an invitation to a social event

ご招待ありがとうございます、お嬢様として参加させていただきます

Go-shōtai arigatō gozaimasu, ojou-sama to shite sanka sasete itadakimasu

Thank you for the invitation, I will attend as a young lady

Apologizing for a minor mistake

お気づかいなくごめんなさい、わたくしの方が遅れていたようです

O-kizuki naku gomen nasai, watakushi no hō ga okureteita yō desu

I'm sorry for the inconvenience, it seems I was the one who was late

In Real Life

⚠️ While it's essential to be respectful and polite in real-life interactions, using the Ojou-sama speech style may come across as overly formal or even pretentious in everyday conversations. Be mindful of your audience and adjust your language accordingly to avoid unintended impressions.
